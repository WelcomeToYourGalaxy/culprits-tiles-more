# Global Coal Mine Tracker download

Global Energy Monitor's download (CC BY 4.0; credit "Global Energy Monitor,
Global Coal Mine Tracker"), sent by the owner on 5 October 2026 and packed to
fit GitHub's upload limit (round 176b). Nothing in the data was left out:

- Global Coal Mine Tracker, August 2026.xlsx: as downloaded.
- Coal Mine Boundaries and Methane Sources - version 1.0.2.zip: the
  all-mines GeoJSON and spreadsheet and the README. The 500 one-mine files
  were left out: GEM's README says they repeat the all-mines files mine by
  mine.
- Global Energy Ownership Tracker September 2026 V2 - Coal Mine
  Ownership.zip: that tracker's Coal Mine Ownership sheet as CSV (26,867
  rows), every column. The tracker's other sheets (plants, pipelines and so
  on) were not needed here.

To update: put a new download here, then run Actions > refresh with
`gem_coal` in the box.
