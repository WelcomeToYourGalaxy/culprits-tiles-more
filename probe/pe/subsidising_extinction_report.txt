Call for action
Development Finance Institutes mandated
to alleviate poverty are instead subsidising
the destruction of natural assets - the most
precious resources for people living in poverty.
This report calls for:
1 Development banks to assess and disclose their
impact on nature and exclude all finance for new
projects linked to industrial livestock, industrial
fishing, fossil fuels, extractives and other sectors that
drive ecosystem destruction through deforestation,
pollution and overfishing.
2 Donor Governments to use their Shareholder and
Director positions to mandate the development banks
they fund to stop providing finance to intermediaries,
companies and projects that destroy nature.
In this report, portfolio.earth
has unveiled four shocking
examples where DFIs are
funding projects that are
destroying rare species and
polluting precious ecosystems.
