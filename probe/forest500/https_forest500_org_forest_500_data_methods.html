Forest 500 data and methods

On this page you can find the assessment and selection methodologies used by Forest 500 and learn how to use and download the full Forest 500 datasets. 

Selection and assessment methodologies

Since 2014, Forest 500 has identified and annually assessed the companies and financial institutions most exposed to deforestation risk in their supply chains and investments on the strength and implementation of their commitments on deforestation and human rights. Two assessment methodologies are used, one for companies and one for financial institutions, but each assesses against best practice.

Learn how we identify the Forest 500 Learn how we assess the Forest 500

Forest 500 data

Forest 500 analyses over 270,000 publicly available data points to evaluate and assess actions taken by 500 companies in the global trade of nine forest risk commodities: beef, cocoa, coffee, leather, palm oil, pulp and paper, rubber, soy and timber. These nine commodities are in the scope of the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR).

Forest 500 looks at over 136,200 publicly available data points to evaluate and assess actions taken by the 150 financial institutions that provide the most financing to Forest 500 companies.

General information on the companies and financial institutions is also included, such as headquarters, sectors they operate in, and at which stages of the supply chain they are exposed to deforestation risk. 

In 2024, as we re-selected the companies and financial institutions, the selection data for producer and trader countries was updated. We also expanded the scope of the assessments in 2024 to include more commodities, forest types and companies.

How to explore the Forest 500 data

All Forest 500 data is publicly available on our website. You can download it above, or view it on our Rankings page. On the Rankings page, you can use filters to explore specific commodities, segments or sectors. You can also click through to company profiles to explore individual assessments in more detail.

Forest 500 data in Forest IQ

Forest IQ offers open access to the best available data on how companies and financial institutions address their links to deforestation. Since 2023 Forest 500 data has been integrated into Forest IQ data on deforestation exposure, financial materiality and performance reporting.

Forest IQ is particularly useful for financial institutions to assess and monitor deforestation risk in their portfolios. In addition, the platform is used by NGOs and media to inform analysis and develop campaigns on deforestation.
