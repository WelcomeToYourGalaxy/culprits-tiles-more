Stories from the Frontlines

Climate change hits the frontlines first and worst. People living on the frontlines of climate chaos and the fossil fuel industry are predominantly Indigenous Peoples, Black and Brown communities, low-wage workers, women, fishers or smallholder farmers, often living in poverty. The featured frontline stories highlight people opposing fossil fuels in their communities, showing the world how to rise to the challenge of this moment.

Data Overview

This report adds up financial commitments – lending and underwriting of debt and equity issuances – from the world’s 65 biggest banks for the fossil fuel sector as a whole, as well as for companies expanding fossil fuels and companies active in specific sectors. Data is based on analysis from the Banking on Climate Chaos (BOCC) research which is derived using multiple sources including Bloomberg LP. Read our Methodology FAQ

Total Fossil Fuel Financing

Financing across all sectors, adjusted for the proportion of business each company does in fossil fuels.

Expansion

Bank financing for 860 oil, gas, and coal companies expanding fossil fuels in 2025, based on research by Urgewald for the Global Oil & Gas Exit List 2025 and the Global Coal Exit List 2025. See methodology document download for full scope and source.

Upstream Oil & Gas Expansion

Bank financing for 330 companies which are actively expanding upstream oil & gas operations. These are companies taking steps to bring new upstream oil, natural gas, condensate, and/or natural gas liquids into production and/or have spent capex on oil and/or gas exploration within the previous three years of financing. Company selection is based on research by Urgewald for the Global Oil & Gas Exit List 2025. See company list document download for full list of companies.

Midstream Oil & Gas Expansion

Bank financing for 254 companies which are developing new oil and/or gas pipelines or which are developing new LNG export capacity. Company selection is based on research by Urgewald for the Global Oil & Gas Exit List 2025. See company list document download for full list of companies.

Gas & Oil Power Expansion

Bank financing for 289 companies which are developing new oil- or gas-fired power plants. Company selection is based on research by Urgewald for the Global Oil & Gas Exit List 2025. See company list document download for full list of companies.
